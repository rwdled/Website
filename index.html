Bio

Skate rat by day, selector by night.

Raised in Corpus Christi and now based in Edinburg, TX, Skte8 is a UTRGV student who picked up the decks in the summer of 2026 and hasn't stepped off since. In that short time Skte8 has already played The Gremlin.

The sound: rolling house grooves, driving techno and big EDM moments, mixed with the same no-rules energy as a session at the local spot. Skte8 is looking for more nights across the RGV and the Coastal Bend: clubs, house parties, campus events and skate jams.
